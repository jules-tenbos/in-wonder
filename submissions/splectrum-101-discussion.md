## 2026-09-07 — Jules's reframe of the opening picture

The first prose pass turned bullets into flowing language without enough emphasis on the overall picture. The essence to lead with:

**Reality disclosed in language.**

- Whenever we have experience of reality — what is around us — there is *already meaning attached*. Things have names; there is a sense of relationships between things.
- This runs through the senses — sound, vision, touch, taste. Draw attention to the fact that we experience reality *through our senses* — and that even there, meaning is already attached. A language that goes beyond speech / natural language.
- Our reality is always already coloured by the way we look at it, interpret it. **There is no objective reality at the point of experience.**
- **Counter-image:** we are NOT blind persons touching a statue, trying to imagine how it looks. (The reconstruction picture — assembling an objective object from poor input — is exactly what disclosure denies.)
- **The positive image:** reality *engages with us* in our language — it speaks what we understand.
- The engagement has **rules** — one can think of it as a game: **language game**. In SPLectrum, language is the medium of exchange with everything needed — concepts AND rules — to 'play the game'.
- Our lives are full of those 'how to's, rules of games: how to be a taxi driver, a policeman, a husband, a mother… **that** is language, in the phrase *reality disclosed in language*. (Roles as games; the everyday face of the broad language concept.)

## 2026-09-07 — the generalisation, early and simple

Passage-per-passage rework agreed; opening draft approved for now. Next move: take the language games away from the anthropocentric, EARLY on the page and in a simple way:

- The games apply to all, not just humans. Animals interacting with their environment, plants too — all have **structured engagement**, all are **playing their game of life**.
- Even dead objects do. (Guard: do not let "game of life" phrasing imply dead objects are alive — the structure of engagement generalises; aliveness does not.)
- Once the generalisation is made, THEN the pivot question: **what do all these languages have in common?** That is what SPLectrum wants to talk about before anything else — what is common to the experience — before unpacking any specific realities.
- Page order principle this sets: generalise first, then the common, then (only then) specifics.

## 2026-09-07 — the two faces reformulated; the sort; the page IS the coin

Jules's reformulation of the coin for the 101: **(1) what do we have in common by the fact that we exist; (2) what can we say about the experience of being in existence.** All other points sorted along those two lines first.

**Structural consequence (agreed): the page itself becomes the coin** — part one answers question 1, part two answers question 2; the coin is named at the hinge ("you've just seen both faces"), not described from outside.

**The sort:**
- **Face 1 (common by existing):** meaning already attached / no objective reality at point of experience (done); everything has its language (done); cognition (the process); experience private as structural fact; sharing → common understanding → knowledge steadies; everything through others (incl. religious belief — the human case of a common mechanism); equal standing (falls out of this face).
- **Face 2 (the experience of being in existence — SPLectrum's own voice):** the three core values; the two realms (realisation / coexistence); the agency case; the realise-pun as last word.
- **Flags:** privacy appears on BOTH faces — fact (face 1) vs value (face 2) — the cleanest demonstration of "re-expressed in content vocabulary"; one hinge sentence to say so. The old draft mis-filed the values on face 1; per the coin page they are face 2's core vocabulary.
- **Host stance sits at the hinge** — the attitude of holding both faces, on neither.

**Ordering decision (Jules):** introduce the coin AT the pivot, before unpacking the common. Then put the emphasis on the **dynamic** — this is not a static model described from outside; it is a **process lived from within** — flowing directly into cognition.

## 2026-09-07 — the bidirectional

By shifting the emphasis to process we end up in the **bidirectional**: we 'speak back'. We don't only absorb — we **emit back**. Deliberately NOT called agency at this point (the word is held for face 2 / the wider agency-telescopic discussion); at the common level it is simply the two-way character of playing: every player changes the game it plays. Note the imagery dividend: the examples already in play turn bidirectional for free — the blackbird changes the lawn it reads; the river carves the bed that then constrains it; you don't just read a room, you walk into it and the room adjusts.

## 2026-09-07 — face 2 correction: the reference point, not fact-to-value

The "each value is a fact from the first face, stood up for" approach rejected. **The difference between the faces is where the reference point is: on the common face it is the language medium; on the own-voice/content face it is the subject.** On the subject face the STARTING point is the three core values (of the subject), supported by the seed principles — not derived on-page from face-1 facts. (Matches the coin page's medium-centred vs subject-centred.) Page hinge reworked accordingly: the crossing = the reference point moving from medium to subject; values then stated as what matters, subject-centred.
