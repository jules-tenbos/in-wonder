# SPLectrum from the foundations

*Working draft. The first part states what is held as common; the second reads several fields through it in SPLectrum's own voice. The two parts are written in different keys on purpose.*

---

## Part one — the common

### Three blocks and one question

Philosophy tends to keep three things in separate rooms. There is the subject matter: reality, what is talked about. There are the languages used to talk about it — natural languages, formal languages, the vocabularies of disciplines. And there are the subjects doing the talking, with whatever couples them to their languages: minds, competences, communities of use. Metaphysics takes the first room, philosophy of language the second, philosophy of mind and epistemology the third.

What held the three rooms together, for a long time, was representation. Language represents the world; subjects use language to reach it. Take representation away — and there are good reasons, walked elsewhere, to take it away — and the rooms are left standing with nothing between them. They can stay apart, each with its own apparatus. Or the partition can be asked a question it was never built to answer.

The question is: where do languages come from?

Within the partition, language is simply there — a third thing sitting between subjects and subject matter, its origin farmed out to a faculty, a convention, or a cultural history. Each of those answers keeps language standalone by giving it a source outside the coupling of subjects. Ask the question without that assumption and the answer is plainer. Languages are what coupling leaves behind. Subjects express, others take up what is expressed, and what recurs sediments into something shareable. The shared is downstream of the individual, not prior to it.

Once that is seen, the third room turns out not to be a room. Communication language resolves into the coupling; the coupling operates on languages already held by subjects; the subject matter is what those languages express. One dynamic, seen from three sides.

### What remains

What is needed to say this is small. A short set of how-constraints, none of them a claim about what exists.

**Being is tension.** Anything that is, is a being held against an other across an interface it maintains. The cut is the primitive; the two sides are constituted by it. (This is P0, the first principle.)

**Historicity.** What passes through a being leaves a trace. The trace shapes what passes next. No two beings carry the same history, because no two occupy the same position.

**Mixing.** Retained traces do not sit still. They recombine, and what they produce re-enters as material for further recombination. Finite material, unbounded mixture-space, outputs folding back as inputs.

**Sedimentation.** Where beings couple, what recurs across their mixing settles into something shared. Languages, at any scale, are this sediment.

**Coupling.** The other side of a cut answers back. What passes through a being is not chosen by the being alone; it is what the other yields under contact — resisting some moves, admitting others, answering differently to different probes. The trace a being retains is shaped by that answering as much as by the being's own history. The other constrains what can be expressed without ever supplying the expression.

Nothing here restricts the dynamic to human beings. That restriction — language happens where humans are — is carried silently by nearly all philosophy, and it is an addition, not a default. The language-game notion is a practice in which moves settle into regularities that participants are held to. Nothing in it says the participants must be human. Strike the clause and the same notion runs through cellular signalling and chemical coupling as readily as through conversation. What is left is the minimal set: the language game with the anthropocentric rider removed.

### A foundation that yields plurality

Here is the shape of the position, and it is the reason the common is worth stating at all.

Foundations usually aim at convergence — one reality, the foundation as the road to it. Pluralisms usually decline foundations; Goodman's worldmaking, Rorty's vocabularies, Putnam's conceptual relativity all reach plurality by refusing to ground. The common above does something neither does. It grounds, and plurality follows from the ground.

The mechanism is the third room. As long as coupling sits outside language and reality — subjects standing apart, using one to reach the other — a single reality can be posited and the subject's contribution treated as interference. Bring the coupling inside, make it constitutive and history-laden, and plurality of realities is no longer an option to be argued for. Each subject articulates from its own sediment; the sediments differ because the histories differ; what each subject has as reality is its expression in the languages it holds. There is no privileged subset stamped as the real one, because there is no position from which the stamping could be done.

So the common is foundational, and its result is that every voice — including the one stating the common — is one voice among others. That is not a concession made afterwards. It is what the foundation produces.

## Part two — own voice

*What follows are readings. They take the common into fields that normally run their own apparatus and see what it does there. Another voice could read these fields differently and the common would stand unaltered; that is the plurality it predicts, showing up in the structure of this document.*

### Reality

There is no reality as a whole, standing behind the realities subjects articulate. Not because it is unreachable, but because a whole would be a being with no other — no interface, no cut, nothing held against anything. The first principle rules it out on its own terms. The universe is the medium in which cuts arise, at every scale, in countless instances; it is not itself a cut.

This is the first appearance of a move that recurs. Refuse the totality; insist on the cut. The same refusal will do work twice more below.

### Aliveness

A system is alive to the degree that it is self-constituting: it maintains the cut that makes it a being, and it remixes what accumulates inside it so that the products re-enter as material. Open-ended evolution falls out of this as a consequence rather than standing as a separate criterion — a system built to fold outputs back as inputs cannot not evolve.

Aliveness so read is graded and substrate-neutral. The fuzziness at the edges — pre-biotic mineral chemistry, viruses, the mule — is not a gap in knowledge; it is what a gradient is. A fuller treatment is sketched separately; the point here is that the reading uses nothing beyond the common.

### Truth, objectivity, correctness

Three notions that ordinarily need three theories get one treatment. Truth is what converges among the subjects coupled in the game currently in play. Objectivity is convergence that holds across broad coupling. Correctness is a move that tracks the convergence. None of this is opinion: convergence is a fact about the game, not a preference of anyone in it. And none of it is indexed to a reality outside the game, because there is none to index to.

The reading scales down as far as the common does. A misfolded protein is wrong relative to the cell it sits in — it fails to converge with the context and is degraded. No new primitive for normativity is needed.

The contrast to draw is with convergence at the limit of inquiry, the ideal community that would eventually agree. That community has no boundary, and a context without a boundary is not a context. The third refusal of the totality: there is no limit to converge toward, only the games there are, each bounded by where coupling actually reaches. Boundaries move as coupling changes. Convergence is always within a cut that could have been drawn elsewhere and will be later.

### Meaning

Meaning does not sit in the head, and it does not sit in the world. It lives in the circulation between subjects.

Each subject holds its own understanding of a word — its own running, reached by its own history, inaccessible in its exactness to anyone else. Nobody compares inner meanings; what gets compared are moves. Common meaning is like convergence in use: enough of it, across enough occasions, and the shared is usable, without any inner exactness ever being shared. The game holds the meaning; the players hold places in it.

Two relocations tempt from here, in opposite directions. The private remainder can be hardened into inner objects — meanings as things in a theatre. The shared surplus can be hardened into an outer fixture — reference, secured by the world past every game. Both take meaning out of the circulation, the one inward, the other outward. Both are the third room reappearing: subjects and world held apart, with meaning needing a home in one of them because the coupling was never given a room.

The world's contribution survives the reading. Things couple differently, and the difference is real before any game articulates it — the other constrains what can be meant without supplying the meaning. Terrain constrains paths; it does not draw the map.

### Belonging

Everything done in a game has belonging attached. To be correct is to belong to the convergence; to deviate is to put belonging at stake. This is why allegiance to a position can feel like faith — it is belonging-in-a-game experienced from inside as truth. It is also why deviation comes first. Every convergence is the sediment of moves that were, when made, incorrect by the standing convergence. Truth is downstream of error.

The other two values, privacy and creativity, enter here too and are treated in their own places.

## The status of the common

The readings in part two are one voice's. So, at the meta-level, is the common itself: one chosen way of cutting, knowingly among others. This does not weaken it. A common whose content is that every subject articulates from its own history predicts that the common, seen from another vocabulary, will show up as one voice among many. The status is entailed, not conceded.

Minimalism is what keeps this from hardening. Positions that carry heavy inventory — physical stuff as basic, abstract objects as real, a mind-independent world as the standard — spend most of their time justifying the inventory rather than using it, and the justifying never ends because nothing forces the inventory. A short set of how-constraints has less to be loyal to. What there is gets tested by what it yields, and the yield is read, each time, in a voice that knows itself to be one among others.
