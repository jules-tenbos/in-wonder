# Semantic Externalism

See [Putnam](/positioning/persons/p/putnam/) for the position in full — Twin Earth, "meanings just ain't in the head," and the division of linguistic labour.

Semantic externalism holds two things together. Meaning outruns the individual head: what a speaker means is not settled by their inner state alone. And the surplus lives partly in the world: on Twin Earth, where the watery stuff is XYZ rather than H₂O, the twins' inner states are identical and their words mean different things — the difference contributed by the stuff itself. The first claim resonates deeply with SPLectrum's structural picture. The fence runs through the second: not whether meaning outruns the head, but *where* the surplus lives. The field itself splits along this line — social externalism, [Tyler Burge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyler_Burge)'s arthritis case, has the community's usage fixing meaning; natural-kind externalism, Twin Earth, has the stuff fixing it — and the fence here is with the natural-kind wing. The social wing belongs with the discovery below.

---

## The discovery

The division of linguistic labour is the strand's genuine find. A speaker uses "elm" and "molybdenum" correctly while unable to tell an elm from a beech or the metal from most others; the load is carried across the community — experts, deference, roles. Meaning held in the shared, individuals coupled to it from their positions: the game holds the meaning, and the players hold places in the game.

The find sharpens when the individual side is stated with equal care. Each head does carry an understanding — its own, built from its own history, and inaccessible in its exactness to anyone else. Nobody compares inner meanings directly; what gets compared are moves. Common understanding therefore amounts, in practice, to convergence in use — not sameness of inner state verified from outside — and *enough* of it makes the shared usable. [Wittgenstein](/positioning/persons/w/wittgenstein/)'s beetle in the box (1953) is the classic form: the word functions in the language whatever the boxes hold. "Red" works this way. The common understanding of red converges across traffic lights and paint charts; each subject's exact understanding is its own running, reached by its own history; and the game never needed the inner exactness to be shared, only the use to converge.

Two reifications tempt from here, at opposite poles. The private remainder can be hardened into inner objects — qualia, the felt redness as a thing alongside the seeing — a move that turns the privacy of a running process into a private inventory; the beetle was built to deflate it, and [Dennett](/positioning/persons/d/dennett/)'s quining of qualia presses the same point from the philosophy of mind. And the shared surplus can be hardened into an outer fixture — reference, secured by the world past every game. Externalism is the second reification. The two are the same move, made in opposite directions: meaning relocated out of the circulation between subjects, inward into a theatre or outward into the world.

## The crux — 1750

Putnam's own test case carries the fence's whole weight. In 1750, before chemistry, no speaker on either planet could distinguish the liquids; the communities' water-games were identical in reach. Externalism says the meanings nonetheless differed — Earth-"water" already referred to H₂O, Twin-"water" to XYZ. That is meaning no member held, no sharing created, and no game was held to — community-independent meaning.

The attribution is backdated. "Their word tracked H₂O all along" is a sentence of *our* chemistry-game, exported into mouths that had no such game — one community's meaning projected onto another and then read back as the world's own contribution. The standing reply — that reference is not understanding, that the causal chain fixes what the word tracks with understanding aside — runs on the machinery it sets aside. Tracking is an achievement: re-identifying the same across occasions. Causal contact alone underdetermines what got baptised — the lump, the metal, the colour, the pointing — and the *qua* that selects among them is understanding, operative at the anchor. Every later link of the chain is an act of use inside a game, understanding at each hand-off. What remains without any of it is bare causal connectedness — a relation that holds between everything and everything nearby.

## The colouring

The intuition feels inevitable inside a particular architecture: world, language, and subjects held in separate rooms, with language needing its standing from somewhere outside the coupling of subjects. A faculty, a convention, a cultural history are the usual donors; the causal chain is a fourth. Within that partition, meaning that outruns the head *must* be lodged in the world — there is nowhere else on the map. The observation is real: meaning does outrun the head. The narrative of the pathway mis-locates where the work happens: in the circulation — subjects expressing, others taking up, what recurs sedimenting into the shared — which the partition has no room for because it was never given a room.

## What the world still does

Setting reference-fixing aside does not zero the world's contribution. XYZ and H₂O couple differently — they behave differently, resist differently, would answer differently to any probe. That difference was real in 1750, present in the relational field as coupling, articulated when a game grew the chemistry to sediment it. The world constrains meaning the way terrain constrains paths: genuinely, at every step, without ever drawing the map. The observation externalism guards — you cannot mean just anything, the stuff pushes back — survives intact. What does not survive is the world doing the meaning's work by itself, past every game in play.

---

The strand keeps its discovery and loses its relocation: meaning outruns the individual head because it lives in the circulation between heads — held privately by each, usable through convergence, answerable to what couples — not because the world holds it for us.
