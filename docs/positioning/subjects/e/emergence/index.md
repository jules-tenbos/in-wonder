# Emergence

The emergence subject gathers the resources that address the emergence question *directly* — the word "emergence" where it is used by name. The word runs across physics, complexity science, biology, the social sciences, artificial intelligence, and philosophy, and the disciplines do not share a definition: each has a locally stable working sense, and the cross-discipline word is contested. The bundle's guiding questions are accordingly about the word itself: what does each definition try to say, how well is it defined and in which language, and where does it make sense. The bundle grows as those questions are worked.
