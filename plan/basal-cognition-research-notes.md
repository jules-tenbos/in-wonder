## The cases, with replication status

- **E. coli chemotaxis — SAFE (textbook-solid since Berg/Koshland, 1970s).** Runs ~1s, tumbles ~0.1s; the cell compares attractant concentration now vs a few seconds ago via methylation-based adaptation (CheR/CheB) on its chemoreceptors — a genuine molecular record of the recent past. Care: "memory" is technical shorthand for delay chemistry, not psychological retention — frame it so.
- **Physarum polycephalum — SAFE.** Maze shortest-path (Nakagaki, Yamada & Tóth, *Nature* 407:470, 2000; Ig Nobel 2008). Tokyo rail (Tero et al., *Science* 327:439, 2010): oat flakes at 36 station positions, the plasmodium converges on a network comparable to the real rail system in efficiency/fault-tolerance/cost; Ig Nobel 2010; spawned a Physarum-inspired-algorithms literature. Both widely reproduced. **Anticipation** (Saigusa et al., *PRL* 100:018101, 2008): periodic cold-dry shocks; mould slows at the time the next (omitted) shock was due — real but probabilistic (~half of individuals in replications); frame as "many, not all."
- **Mimosa habituation — CONTESTED.** Gagliano et al., *Oecologia* 2014 (drops, response waning, novel-stimulus control, 28-day retention). Critique: Biegler, *Oecologia* 2018 (no proper dishabituation control; fatigue not excluded). **Keep separate from the pea-plant claim:** Markel 2020 (eLife) was a failed replication of Gagliano's *pea Pavlovian learning* (2016), not Mimosa; Gagliano's eLife reply argues Markel's CS was ineffective. Field status: unresolved (cf. *Nature Plants* "Go forth and replicate" editorial, 2020). Only tellable AS a replication-controversy story.
- **Ciliates — SAFE and story-rich.** *Stentor roeselii*: Dexter, Prabakaran & Gunawardena, *Current Biology* 2019 replicated Jennings 1906 — under repeated irritant a hierarchy of escape responses (bend → reverse cilia → contract → detach); discredited for decades by a 1967 non-replication that used the wrong species/stimulus; the 2019 team succeeded with plastic microbeads after carmine powder failed. **Gelber/Paramecium**: Gershman, Balbi, Gallistel & Gunawardena, eLife 2021, rehabilitate Beatrice Gelber's 1950s conditioning experiments — the classic dismissals were flawed; status is historical re-evaluation plus a call to re-run, not yet a modern replication.

## Gap-closing round (2026-08-25) — quorum sensing + pre-neural bioelectricity

### Quorum sensing — the bacterial interaction language

- **The mechanism (textbook-solid).** Autoinducers synthesised, released, detected; above threshold concentration (= population density) reception flips gene expression population-wide, synchronously. Founding line: Nealson & Hastings, *Vibrio fischeri* (1970); "quorum sensing" coined Fuqua, Winans & Greenberg (1994); standard reviews Miller & Bassler (*Annu Rev Microbiol* 2001), Waters & Bassler (2005).
- **Dialects and a common language — the field's own vocabulary.** Species-specific autoinducers (AHL family Gram-negative, oligopeptides Gram-positive) intraspecies; **AI-2** (Bassler et al. 1994, *V. harveyi*) produced/detected across Gram-negative AND Gram-positive species — standardly described as a "universal" interspecies signal, "a common language for bacterial interaction" (the field's phrase). Receptor diversity (LuxP, LsrB, dCACHE) → the meaning of the signal is fixed on the reception side.
- **Regulates:** bioluminescence, virulence, competence, motility, antibiotic production, **biofilm formation** (collectively created structure — sediment in shared physical form).
- **Bassler's combinatorial point:** *V. harveyi* integrates multiple parallel circuits — distinguishing "how many of us" from "how many of them."

### Pre-neural bioelectricity — the sourcing

- **Primary backing:** Keijzer, van Duijn & **Lyon**, "What nervous systems do: early evolution, input–output, and the skin brain thesis," *Adaptive Behavior* 21(2), 2013 (Lyon co-author — the field's conceptual architect on the pre-neural line).
- **Levin's statement:** "The computational boundary of a 'self'" (*Front Psychol* 2019) — all cells electrically active; neurons evolved from pre-neural precursors already using ion-based signalling; nervous systems as speed-optimisation of the ancestral medium.
- **Honest breadth:** the descent claim (neural signalling from pre-existing cellular bioelectricity) is mainstream and safe; *why* nervous systems arose is disputed — skin brain thesis is one proposal in an acknowledged option space (Jékely, Keijzer & Godfrey-Smith, *Phil Trans R Soc B* 2015). Lean on descent only.
