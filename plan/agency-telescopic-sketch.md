## Governing formulations (Jules, verbatim-in-substance)

- **The telescopic dimension** *(2026-09-06)*: cognition serves a purpose — a telescopic dimension — though from the creature's point of view what the acting may be for is not visible.
- **Direction in the landscape** *(2026-09-06)*: the direction of evolution is embedded in the landscape, not the agents.
- **The governing image** *(2026-09-06)*: headless chickens running across a landscape with hidden, embedded intelligence — intelligence in the sense of opportunity. Who can find the box with the prize — but done in a headless / random-approach way. NOT purposes hidden inside creatures: the intelligence is the landscape's opportunity structure; the agents are blind searchers; the prize is found by running, not by seeing.
- **The knowledge-and-understanding check, folded in** *(2026-09-06)*: the basal round's open question — does the bacterial case carry both knowledge and understanding (the held and the operative), or only the held side — becomes, in this setting: **is understanding for its own sake meaningful in this evolutionary setting?** In the headless-landscape picture, understanding would seem to exist for the run (operative, in service of traversal); whether "for the sake of it" has any standing — or is itself a late artefact of one lineage's language — is for this thread to settle.

## The central joint (unresolved — the thread's likely thesis-site)

**Refined to a three-part picture (Jules, 2026-09-09):** (1) a **dynamic landscape** with hidden opportunities — dynamic partly because the agents' own running reshapes it; (2) agents that behave **a bit like** headless chickens at landscape scale; (3) yet the agents carry **internal world models — language games** — and use cause-and-effect patterns to help with decisions, and may claim to be rational for it. The middle layer is where the thread's originality lives: model-guided locally, headless globally — the models built from each agent's own sediment, aimed at local grip, blind to the boxes; the rationality claim as the cocoon again (a game's cause-effect grip mistaken for sight of the landscape).

The earlier two-picture form is the same joint before the refinement: **headless search at the landscape scale** (evolution; the chickens; direction in the terrain) and **directed search within a lifetime** (the bacterium climbing its gradient; the child reaching — nothing headless there). The interesting question: how directed local cognition rides on headless landscape-scale search — and whether "telescopic" names precisely the relation between the two scales. A version of Darwinian evolution formulated from this joint would locate selection's directionality in the landscape's opportunity structure while keeping the agents' within-lifetime cognition fully directed — two scales, one process, no teleology imported at either.

## Research anchors (unscoped — the survey the round will need)

- **Teleonomy** — the field that fought the purpose-vocabulary battle: Mayr's teleonomic/teleomatic distinction, Monod's chance and necessity.
- **Okasha** — agents and goals in evolution: when agential thinking about organisms earns its keep and when it misleads.
- **Dennett** — free-floating rationales: reasons in the design without a reasoner.
- **Levin** — the cognitive light cone; TAME's agency scale; degrees of freedom.
- **Fitness-landscape literature** — the landscape formalism itself (Wright onward), its uses and its critics (static-landscape objections; coevolving landscapes — the terrain moved by the runners, which the bidirectional predicts).
- **World models and bounded rationality** — the middle layer's own literature: Simon's bounded rationality and satisficing; the heuristics-and-biases and ecological-rationality dispute (Kahneman vs Gigerenzer); causal reasoning as the model's engine (Pearl); predictive processing / generative models (Friston, already on-site); "world models" in current AI. The rationality *claim* vs the rationality *achieved* is its own research seam.
- Counter-sourcing note: the agential-thinking literature self-advocates in both directions (eliminativists and enthusiasts); weight the critics of whichever framing the thread adopts.
